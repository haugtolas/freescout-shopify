# Changelog

## 1.0.1

- The order panel follows the colors of the Atelier theme (light and dark, any palette). Before, it could stay light on a dark Atelier page until the page was reloaded.
- Dark pages are detected more reliably (theme markers, color scheme, background, then text color).
- The customer phone number copied from Shopify is now labelled Mobile instead of Work.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Shopify customer and latest orders in the conversation sidebar, with an order details panel.
- Customer sync: empty fields of the FreeScout customer are filled from Shopify.
- Clickable order numbers in messages and an order search box.
- Connection with a Shopify Dev Dashboard app (client credentials), or a legacy access token.
- One store for the whole helpdesk or one store per mailbox.
- Workflows conditions and actions, and Shopify customer tags as conversation tags (Tags module).
- Optional restriction of order lookups to the customer of the conversation.
- English, French, German, Spanish and Italian.
